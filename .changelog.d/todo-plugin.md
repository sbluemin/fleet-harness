### fleet-console
#### Added
- Added an Objectives surface: each objective is carried out by missions that form a lineup (a dependency graph), led by a Commander Operation. Plan asks the Commander to lay out the missions, Commence tells it to proceed, and it delegates missions to named assignee sessions when it sees fit; the person reviews and completes the objective. Every agent Operation is an objective: an Operation started anywhere in the Console appears in Objectives, and a new objective adds its Commander to the sidebar as a dormant Operation that wakes when you plan or commence. Once every mission is done and the Commander has confirmed each success criterion written below the missions with evidence, the objective moves to review on its own and gathers in its own section at the top of the list. While the Commander works, or while the objective waits for review, you can still add missions and edit the brief, then press Steer to have it re-read the objective and fit the new missions into the lineup; images attached to the brief reach the Commander as files it can open. Each time the Commander marks a mission done it leaves a short conclusion-first record on that mission, and reworking a mission adds another, so you can open a mission and read what happened there in time order; new records are marked until you read them. Delegated missions run in the background: the Commander's panel stands for the whole objective, and the buttons in its corner switch it to any mission's session. Open it with Cmd+Shift+Y (Ctrl+Shift+Y on Windows and Linux), keep it docked in the right rail beside your Operations or expand it over the canvas, and drag objectives to reorder them; its layout fits the width it has.
