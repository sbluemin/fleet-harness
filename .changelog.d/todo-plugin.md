### fleet-console
#### Added
- Added a To-do surface: items with steps that form a recipe (a dependency graph), each run by a Chef Operation. Cook asks the Chef to plan, Start tells it to proceed, and it delegates steps to named assignee sessions when it sees fit; the person reviews and completes the item. While the Chef works, or after it hands the item back for review, you can still add steps and edit the note, then press Steer to have it re-read the item and fit the new steps into the recipe; images attached to the note reach the Chef as files it can open. Each time the Chef marks a step done it leaves a short conclusion-first record on that step, and reworking a step adds another, so you can open a step and read what happened there in time order; new records are marked until you read them. Delegated steps run in the background: the Chef's panel stands for the whole item, and the buttons in its corner switch it to any step's session. Open it with Cmd+Shift+Y (Ctrl+Shift+Y on Windows and Linux), keep it docked in the right rail beside your Operations or expand it over the canvas, and drag items to reorder them; its layout fits the width it has.
