### fleet-console
#### Added
- Added an Objectives surface: each objective is carried out by missions that form a lineup (a dependency graph), led by a Commander Operation. A single action at the bottom of an objective follows its state: Plan asks the Commander to lay out the missions and Commence tells it to proceed, and you can add a few words for the Commander before sending either; the person reviews and completes the objective, which puts its Commander and member sessions to sleep so you can resume them later. An objective can have a roster of members (roles such as research or review, each with a short description and a model that defaults to AI Gateway routing), and each mission names the member who does it; when the roster is empty, the Commander proposes members as it plans. Commence starts every member at once as a waiting session that costs nothing until the Commander sends it work, and a member keeps its context across the missions it takes. Once started, each member shows its actual model by a friendly name with a colored provider glyph, independently of its model-selection setting. Every agent Operation is an objective: an Operation started anywhere in the Console appears in Objectives, and a new objective adds its Commander to the sidebar as a dormant Operation that wakes when you plan or commence. Choose Terminal or Chat when adding an objective, or change its starting view before the Commander first runs; the next objective remembers your last choice. Member sessions stay out of the sidebar, the palette, mentions, the War Room and Quaker Aides notifications; the Commander stands for them, and on mobile you answer a member's question from the desktop or web Console. Once every mission is done and the Commander has confirmed each success criterion written above the missions with evidence, the objective moves to review on its own and gathers in its own section at the top of the list. While the Commander works, or while the objective waits for review, you can still add missions and edit the brief, then press Steer, optionally with a note, to have it re-read the objective and fit the new missions into the lineup; images attached to the brief reach the Commander as files it can open. Each time the Commander marks a mission done it leaves a short conclusion-first record on that mission, and reworking a mission adds another, so you can open a mission and read what happened there in time order; new records are marked until you read them. Members work in the background: the Commander's panel stands for the whole objective, and the buttons in its corner, named after each member, switch it to that member's session, and the caption's tools, such as the Browser, follow the member you are viewing. With Objectives open in the right rail, selecting a canvas panel or a Fleet Map dot opens its objective without taking keyboard focus from the panel. Open it with Cmd+Shift+Y (Ctrl+Shift+Y on Windows and Linux), keep it docked in the right rail beside your Operations or expand it over the canvas, and drag objectives to reorder them, with the sidebar following the same order, and rename or recolor a group right from the list. An objective reads top to bottom as its roster, brief, success criteria, missions and lineup; long missions and criteria show in full, and the criteria and missions fold. Its layout fits the width it has.
