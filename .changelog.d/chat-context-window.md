### fleet-console
#### Added
- Show how much of the context window a chat session is using. A meter chip sits with the view chips and opens a breakdown of what fills the window: messages, tools, memory files, skills, and free space. Each folded turn line carries the tokens that turn added. The reading is taken while the turn runs, so it is always as of the last turn and says so.
