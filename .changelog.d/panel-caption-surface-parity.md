### fleet-console
#### Changed
- An Operation panel is now one surface. The caption, the border around the terminal, the terminal field, and the chat log all take a single color per theme, so a panel reads as one window instead of three stacked sheets, and Maritime loses the darker ring that used to frame its terminal. The panel still stands clear of the canvas in every theme: brighter than it in the dark themes, darker in Whites. Focus, identity accent, and the status rail keep working as before, now against a single fill.
