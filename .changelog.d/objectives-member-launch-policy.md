### fleet-console
#### Changed
- Objective members no longer stop to ask you questions. They send the decisions they need to their Commander, so the objective keeps moving. A member's terminal session picks this up the next time it starts. If you later switch only the Commander or a member to the other view, messages between them may wait for your approval.
