### fleet-console
#### Changed
- When you ask the Commander to plan an objective, it can now propose adding, revising, or removing success criteria, even if the objective already has some. You approve or reject each proposal on its criterion, or annotate it and plan again. Commence and Steer stay locked until every proposal is decided.
