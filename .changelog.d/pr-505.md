### fleet-console
#### Added
- [fleet-console] Zoom the triage Watch Deck with pinch or Ctrl/Cmd+wheel (0.35x-2.0x, per-theater persistence); zooming out past the card-legibility threshold dissolves the deck into a fleet map with one status-colored marker per operation, and a Density chip on the triage rail shows the zoom and cycles presets.
