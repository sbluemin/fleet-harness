section: Changed
---

- [fleet-console] Set an Operation's accent color by clicking its status indicator — on the panel title bar or on its dock chip — which opens a color popover; the dedicated dock accent button is gone, and the dock indicator lets you accent minimized panels too.
- [fleet-console] An Operation's accent color now outlines its canvas panel as well as its dock chip, taking over the focus outline in the chosen hue and staying visible whether or not the Operation is focused, running, awaiting, or minimized.
